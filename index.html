How to make the full 30 seconds

  1. Open the engine above (free, no payment). Upload your reference photo as the start frame / image input.
  2. Paste the Segment 1 prompt and generate. Download the clip.
  3. For Segment 2, use the last frame of Segment 1 as the start image (save it from the clip) and paste the Segment 2 prompt. Repeat for Segment 3.
  4. Join the clips in any free editor (CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, or ffmpeg) — no re-render needed.
  5. If a segment comes out wrong, regenerate only that segment — never the whole video.

Tip: keep every segment prompt's subject, face, clothes and setting identical. Changing one word of the subject between segments is the most common cause of a face or scene changing mid-video.
