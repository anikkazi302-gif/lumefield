# LumeField Security Policy

## What this covers
The LumeField website (https://anikkazi302-gif.github.io/lumefield/), its Firebase sign-in and database rules, and this repository.

## How to report a security problem
Use GitHub's private **"Report a vulnerability"** button on this repository (Security tab), or open a GitHub issue and mark it private. Do not post the details in public.

A good report tells us: what you found, where it is (page, URL, steps to reproduce), and what an attacker could do with it. Screenshots help.

## What we promise
- We will acknowledge your report within **3 business days**.
- We will triage it within **10 business days** and tell you what we found.
- We will fix confirmed problems as fast as we can and tell you when it is fixed.
- We will not take legal action against anyone who reports a problem in good faith and follows these rules.
- With your permission, we will thank you on the site.

## Rules for researchers
- Test only your own account and data. Never access, download, modify or delete another user's data.
- Never disrupt the service (no denial-of-service, no spam, no phishing).
- Do not disclose the problem publicly until we have fixed it — please give us **90 days** from acknowledgement before any public disclosure.

## No paid bounty
There is no paid bug bounty for LumeField. We will thank you publicly (with your permission) but we cannot pay for reports.

## Honest limits
- LumeField is a static website on GitHub Pages with data on Google Firebase. We run no server of our own.
- We hold no SOC 2 certification and no ISO certification. Your data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure, which is covered by Google Cloud's SOC 2 and ISO 27001 compliance. All pages are served over HTTPS.
- We do not run any AI model and we never train AI on your data.
