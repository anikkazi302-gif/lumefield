Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The short version

LumeField collects almost nothing. You can start without an account — your first video need no sign-in — and nothing you type or upload on this site is sent to us.

What we collect

If you use the tool without signing in, this site collects no personal data at all.

If you sign in (free, email or Google, through Firebase), the only personal data we keep is your login email, your credit balance, and the characters and video plans you choose to save. Your login email is handled by Firebase; your credit balance and saved items are stored in our database (Firestore) under your account, only so the tool knows how many credits you have left and can show your saved characters and videos. We never see your password, and we never sell or share your email or your credit balance.

Your photos stay in your browser

When you upload reference photos in the video tool, they are previewed only in your own browser — never uploaded by this site and never stored by us. The exception is a photo you choose to save as a character in My characters: that photo (downscaled) is stored in our database under your account, together with the name and description you give it, and so are video plans you choose to save in My videos. When you generate a video, you upload the photo yourself on the engine's own page — that is the only place it goes.

Tracking

We do no tracking beyond basic anonymous usage counts, if any — the kind of counts a static host keeps (for example, how many times a page was loaded). There are no advertising trackers, no analytics profiles, and nothing that follows you across the web.

Payments

If you buy extra credits, payment is handled only by our payment provider (Paddle or Polar — the Merchant of Record shown on the checkout page). Your card details go only to the payment provider and are never sent to, seen by, or stored by LumeField. The only payment information we keep is the record we need to add your credits: your email, which credit pack you bought, and your credit balance — stored in our database (Firestore) under your account. We never sell or share that record. The payment provider has its own privacy policy, which applies to the payment itself.

Third-party engines

Your video is rendered on third-party engines (such as Hugging Face Spaces, Kling, Google Flow, or Runway). When you open one of them, you leave this site and that engine's own privacy policy applies — including whatever it collects when you upload a photo or create an account there. Please read the engine's policy before you upload anything to it.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change if the service changes — for example, when the credit system changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will change with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the project on GitHub: github.com/anikkazi302-gif/lumefield.
