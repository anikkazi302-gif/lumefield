Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. A free tool, in plain words

LumeField is a free-to-start tool. It helps you plan a 30-second video from your reference photos and writes the prompts for it. The free plan needs no payment, no subscription, and no card. Extra credits can be bought in packs — see section 8.

2. We don't render your video

This site does not render video itself. Your video is rendered on third-party free engines — for example free Hugging Face Spaces, Kling, Google Flow, or Runway — which you open yourself. Those engines have their own rules, their own free limits, and their own terms. When you use them, their terms apply too.

3. Your prompts and photos are your responsibility

You are responsible for what you write and what photos you use. Do not use LumeField to make anything illegal, hateful, or infringing — no content that breaks the law, harasses or targets people, or uses someone else's photos, faces, or work without permission. Do not use it to make violent, bloody, or gory videos. Only use photos you have the right to use — your own photos, or photos you have permission for.

4. No warranty — the service is provided as-is

LumeField is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind. We cannot promise the site will always be available, that a prompt will produce the video you want, or that any engine will accept your video. The engines decide what they render, and their results are their own.

5. Free limits can change

The free limits shown on this site (for example, how many free credits an engine gives per day) come from the engines, not from us. Free limits can change at any time when the engines change theirs. Always check the engine's own page before planning work around a limit.

6. Accounts

Sign-in is available (email or Google, through Firebase). Your first video needs no account. Accounts may be closed if they are used to abuse the service or break these terms.

7. Credits

Your first video plan is free with no account. After that, sign in (free) and you get 20 credits. Each video plan costs credits: 5 seconds = 4, 10 seconds = 7, 20 seconds = 14, 30 seconds = 20. When your credits run out, the tool shows "Credits finished" and you cannot build more video plans until you have credits again. Your credit balance is stored in our database (Firestore) under your account, and the rules only ever let it go down by the cost of a video you build — nobody can add credits to their own account. Extra credits can be bought in packs (see section 8); bought credits are added to your balance by us after your payment is confirmed, and they stay in your balance until you use them.

8. Buying credits & refunds

Credit packs are sold in US dollars. The packs are: Starter — 60 credits — $4.99; Plus — 150 credits — $9.99; Ultra — 300 credits — $19.99. Prices shown on the site are the final prices once checkout is open; before that, prices shown are proposed only. Payment is handled by our Merchant of Record (Paddle or Polar — the name is shown on the checkout page), who takes your payment on their own secure checkout page, handles sales tax, and pays us what is left. Your card details go only to the payment provider and never touch LumeField — this site never sees, stores, or handles card numbers.

7-day money-back guarantee: if it is your first purchase, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of the purchase, no questions asked, and you get the full price you paid back. After 7 days, or after you have used the credits from that purchase, there is no refund for that purchase. Credit packs are one-time purchases, not subscriptions — there is nothing to cancel and nothing renews. If subscriptions are added later, you will be able to cancel them from the payment provider's customer portal; cancellation stops the next charge and you keep access until the paid period ends.

Refunds are paid by the payment provider to the same payment method you used. We cannot promise a video result — the video is rendered on third-party free engines, not by us — so a refund is for the credits, not for the video an engine produced.
